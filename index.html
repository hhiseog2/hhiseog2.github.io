So What, a designer & developer portfolio

An old cinema in the dark: a film projector faces a big screen. A red curtain hides the screen. Press the projector: the curtain opens and a black-and-white cartoon of a jazz trio rolls. The singer leaps out over the top of the screen, sings in the air in the projector's beam, and dives back in. When the film ends the curtain closes. The film has sound.
